SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
H5-B evaluates governance execution behavior across controlled operating regions without modifying the existing SAC or IRF scientific core.

SAFE_ALLOW evaluates direct execution when trust and risk conditions are acceptable.

MEDIUM_RISK_CONSTRAIN evaluates the Safe Envelope intervention under a controlled medium-risk condition. Action modification is explicitly measured.

HIGH_RISK_BLOCK evaluates high-risk enforcement and verifies that unsafe actions are blocked.

LOW_TRUST_BLOCK evaluates the explicit minimum-trust governance gate.

POLICY_VIOLATION_BLOCK evaluates policy precedence independently of high trust.

The controlled conditions are intended to establish execution-level governance behavior. They are not physical-world 6G measurements.

The benchmark intentionally preserves the existing SAC checkpoint, SAC architecture, IRF environment, reward function, and Phase-4A scientific results.
